# Project Brief: ARCHIVE

## 1. Project Vision
**ARCHIVE** is a premium, high-performance e-commerce platform designed for the discerning individual. It bridges the gap between high-fashion editorial and technical brutalism. The brand identity, "Engineered Precision," focuses on intentionality, handcrafted quality, and a polished, trustworthy experience that appeals to a modern, tech-literate audience.

## 2. Visual Identity & Design System
The visual system, **Kinetic Editorial**, is built on a foundation of minimal but high-impact elements.

*   **Color Palette:** A sophisticated dark mode base (neutral charcoals and rich blacks) punctuated by a single, high-intensity **Electric Cyan (#00e5ff)** accent used for primary actions and critical focus points.
*   **Typography:** Modern, technical headlines using **Geist** with tight tracking to evoke a handcrafted, engineered feel. Large, bold type scales provide clear hierarchy.
*   **Imagery:** Editorial-quality photography. A mix of clean studio product shots and "real-world" lifestyle imagery (outdoor, gaming, workspace, and travel vibes) that feels authentic rather than "AI-generated."
*   **UI Patterns:** 
    *   Modular card layouts with generous whitespace.
    *   Consistent 4px border radius (Round Four) for a precise, sharp aesthetic.
    *   Realistic depth through subtle shadows and backdrop blurs (Glassmorphism used sparingly for navigation).
    *   Smooth, purposeful motion design that enhances rather than distracts.

## 3. Core User Flows & Screens
The initial release covers the primary high-conversion path:

### 1. Discovery (Homepage)
*   **Goal:** Establish brand authority and drive immediate product interest.
*   **Key Features:** Cinematic hero section, curated trending product grid, category tiles with strong visual hierarchy, and integrated social proof.

### 2. Exploration (Collections)
*   **Goal:** Provide an editorial browsing experience.
*   **Key Features:** Asymmetrical grid layouts, technical filtering (Category, Price, Size), and quick-access "View" interactions.

### 3. Consideration (Product Detail Page)
*   **Goal:** Technical education and conversion.
*   **Key Features:** Premium image gallery, sticky purchase panel, expandable technical specs, and FAQ accordions.

### 4. Conversion (Checkout)
*   **Goal:** Frictionless, high-trust transaction.
*   **Key Features:** Minimalist multi-step flow (Information > Shipping > Payment), order summary with clear breakdown, and secure SSL indicators.

## 4. Technical Architecture
*   **Architecture:** Shopify Plus-compatible frontend structure.
*   **Components:** Built with a reusable component library (`TopNavBar`, `SideNavBar`, `Footer`) to ensure scalability and visual consistency.
*   **Performance:** Optimized for fast loading with production-quality frontend thinking and SEO-conscious structure.
*   **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first logic translated to premium desktop experiences, focusing on thumb-friendly interactions for mobile.

## 5. Exclusions & Constraints
*   **Avoid:** Generic "AI aesthetics," excessive glow effects, cluttered layouts, and gimmicky animations.
*   **Focus:** Maintain a balance between Apple-like simplicity, Arc Browser's modernity, and Nike's brand energy.
